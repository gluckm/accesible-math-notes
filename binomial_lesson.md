# Binomial Expansion Lesson

Here is our primary multiline equation layout:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(x + y)^2 &= (x + y)(x + y) \\
          &= x^2 + xy + yx + y^2 \\
          &= x^2 + 2xy + y^2
\end{aligned}
$$
